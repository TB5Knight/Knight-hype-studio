Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about working with us.

What is your process?

We follow a structured approach: Discovery, Concept, Creation, Refinement, and Delivery. Each phase includes client collaboration and feedback to ensure alignment.

Do you work with remote clients?

Absolutely. We work with clients worldwide through video calls, email, and project management tools. Distance is never a barrier to great work.

What file formats do you deliver?

We provide files in all standard formats based on your needs - high-resolution print files, web-optimized images, source files, and more.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes, all projects include revision rounds to ensure you're completely satisfied with the final result. Specific details are outlined in our proposals.

Can you match our brand style?

Yes, we're experienced in working within existing brand guidelines while bringing fresh creative perspectives to your projects.

How do payments work?

We typically require a deposit to begin work, with the balance due upon project completion. Payment plans are available for larger projects.
